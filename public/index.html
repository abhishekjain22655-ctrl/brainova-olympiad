Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

  • Objective Format: All questions are 4-choice Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
  • Positive Marks: Each correct response awards +4 marks.
  • No Negative Marking: Unattempted or incorrect answers carry 0 negative marks in preliminary rounds.
  • Proctored Environment: Browser tab switches and full-screen exits are tracked.

National Ranking & Awards

  • National Rank 1 to 3: Gold / Silver / Bronze Medals + Merit Cash Scholarship.
  • Zonal & School Toppers: Gold Medal of Excellence in each partner school tenant.
  • Digital Certificate of Participation: Granted to every verified registered candidate.
  • Performance Analytics: Detailed percentile report card across all subjects.
